Private On-Device Scoring

The model runs in your browser using WebGPU or CPU/WebAssembly. Questionnaire evidence is not sent to a model service. Model files download from Hugging Face and libraries from a CDN; those hosts receive ordinary download metadata.

Model files may be cached locally. Responses and the local result record stay in browser storage for review or retries. Starting a new assessment clears the participant records; clearing browser site data also removes cached models.

Research submission is disabled by default. If an authorised researcher enables it in scoring.yaml, participant demographics, summary scores and model/configuration provenance are sent to the existing Google Sheets endpoint.
